# Passenger No-Show Policy (Version 2026.1)

## 1. No-Show Definition
A passenger is classified as a "No-Show" if they fail to check in or board the aircraft prior to gate closure (15 minutes before scheduled departure) without prior cancellation.

## 2. Penalty & Ticket Forfeiture
- **Standard & Basic Tickets**: In the event of a No-Show, the ticket is completely forfeited with 0% refund value. Subsequent connecting segments on the itinerary will be automatically cancelled.
- **Flex & Business Tickets**: No-Show penalty fee of $150 applies. Remaining ticket value can be converted to flight credit valid for 12 months.

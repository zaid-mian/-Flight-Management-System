# Airline Refund & Escalation Policy (Version 2026.1)

## 1. Refund Processing Timeframes
- Approved cash refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
- Credit voucher refunds are issued electronically within 24 hours.

## 2. Mandatory Human Approval Thresholds
- Any refund calculation exceeding $500.00 USD requires explicit Human Supervisor Approval before payment processing.
- Medical emergency refund requests (requiring doctor verification) require Human Support Review.
- Bereavement refund requests require certificate verification and Human Supervisor Approval.

## 3. Non-Refundable Components
- Optional service fees (e.g., priority boarding, advance seat selection fees, pet transport fees) are non-refundable once the flight has departed.
- Government security taxes are always 100% refundable upon booking cancellation regardless of fare class.

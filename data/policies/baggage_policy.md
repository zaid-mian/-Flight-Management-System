# Airline Baggage Allowance & Fee Policy (Version 2026.1)

## 1. Cabin Carry-On Allowance
- Every passenger is permitted 1 personal item (fits under seat, max 40 x 30 x 15 cm).
- Economy Standard / Economy Flex / Business Class includes 1 carry-on bag (max 10 kg, 55 x 40 x 23 cm).
- Economy Basic permits personal item only; carry-on bag incurs a $35 gate fee if brought to boarding.

## 2. Checked Baggage Allowance & Fees
- **Business Class**: 2 checked bags included free (up to 32 kg / 70 lbs each).
- **Economy Flex**: 1 checked bag included free (up to 23 kg / 50 lbs).
- **Economy Standard / Basic**: First checked bag costs $30 if purchased online >24 hours before flight ($45 at airport counter). Second checked bag costs $50.
- **Overweight / Oversized Baggage**: Bags between 23 kg and 32 kg incur an overweight surcharge of $75. Bags exceeding 32 kg will not be accepted.

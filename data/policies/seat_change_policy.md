# Seat Selection & Seat Change Policy (Version 2026.1)

## 1. Seat Selection Standard Rules
- Standard seat assignment is complimentary during online check-in (opens 24 hours prior to departure).
- Preferred seats (extra legroom, exit rows, front rows) can be selected for an additional fee ranging from $15 to $45 depending on flight duration.

## 2. Seat Upgrade Conditions
- Seat upgrades from Economy to Business Class may be requested up to 4 hours before departure subject to seat availability.
- Upgrade price difference must be paid immediately upon confirmation.
- Waitlisted seat upgrades are automatically processed by score priority if seats become available.

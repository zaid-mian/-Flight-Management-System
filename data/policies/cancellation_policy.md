# Flight Cancellation Policy (Version 2026.1)

## 1. Overview
Passengers may cancel eligible flight bookings subject to ticket fare conditions, timing of request prior to departure, and applicable cancellation fees.

## 2. Cancellation Timeframes & Refund Eligibility
- **24-Hour Grace Period**: Any booking cancelled within 24 hours of purchase is eligible for a 100% full refund with zero cancellation penalty, provided departure is at least 7 days away.
- **More than 48 Hours Before Departure**:
  - Economy Flex / Business Class: Full refund minus a $25 processing fee.
  - Economy Standard: 80% refund of base fare; 20% cancellation fee applied.
  - Economy Basic (Non-Refundable): No cash refund. 50% credit voucher provided.
- **24 to 48 Hours Before Departure**:
  - Economy Flex / Business Class: 90% refund of base fare.
  - Economy Standard: 50% refund of base fare.
  - Economy Basic: Non-refundable, zero refund or credit voucher.
- **Less than 24 Hours Before Departure**:
  - All fare classes incur a 100% cancellation penalty. Only government taxes and unused airport fees are refundable.

## 3. Airline-Initiated Cancellations
If the airline cancels a flight due to weather, mechanical failure, or operational reasons, passengers are entitled to a 100% full cash refund or immediate rebooking on the next available flight at no extra charge.

# Flight Booking Change Policy (Version 2026.1)

## 1. Flight Date & Time Changes
- Flight date/time modifications requested >24 hours before departure:
  - Economy Flex / Business Class: Free change (passenger pays fare difference if new flight is more expensive).
  - Economy Standard: $50 change fee + fare difference.
  - Economy Basic: Flight changes not permitted.
- Modifications requested <24 hours before departure incur a $100 rush change fee across all classes.

## 2. Name Changes & Passenger Corrections
- Minor spelling corrections (up to 3 characters) are processed free of charge upon presenting passport ID.
- Complete passenger name transfers are strictly prohibited due to security security guidelines.
